# Photo credits - VADA inspections demo

All sample photos in this demo are real photographs (no AI images), downloaded from the original image host and resized to max 1600 px JPEG (Unsplash images under the Unsplash License; Pexels images under the Pexels License; Unsplash+ images excluded).

Matched pairs (v2, 12 Nov 2026): so each move-in / move-out pair shows the same kind of surface, the move-in photo of every damaged item is a crop of an undamaged area of the same real photo (or the same type of surface) and the move-out photo is cropped around the damage. Nothing was painted in, retouched or generated.
